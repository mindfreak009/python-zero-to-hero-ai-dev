COURSE_NAME: Data Science and Business Analytics
DURATION: 20 Weeks
LEVEL: Beginner to Intermediate
KEYWORDS: data science, Python, pandas, NumPy, SQL, statistics, visualization, machine learning

OVERVIEW:
This course teaches learners to collect, clean, analyze, visualize, and interpret data using Python, statistics, SQL, and machine learning.

MODULES:
1. Data Science Fundamentals: Data types, data collection, and analytical workflows.
2. Python: Python basics, pandas, and NumPy.
3. Statistics: Probability, averages, distributions, and hypothesis testing.
4. SQL: Queries, joins, filtering, and aggregation.
5. Data Analysis: Missing values, outliers, and exploratory data analysis.
6. Visualization: Charts, dashboards, and business reporting.
7. Machine Learning: Regression, classification, clustering, and model evaluation.

PROJECTS:
- Retail sales analysis
- Customer churn prediction
- House price prediction
- Business performance dashboard

PREREQUISITES:
Basic computer skills and mathematics are recommended. Previous programming experience is not mandatory.

FAQ:
Q: What is EDA?
A: Exploratory Data Analysis examines data using statistics and visualizations to identify patterns, relationships, and quality issues.

Q: What is the difference between classification and regression?
A: Classification predicts categories, while regression predicts numerical values.

Q: Which tools are covered?
A: Python, pandas, NumPy, SQL, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and scikit-learn.
