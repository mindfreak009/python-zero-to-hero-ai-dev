COURSE_NAME: Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI
DURATION: 16 Weeks
LEVEL: Beginner to Intermediate
KEYWORDS: AI, machine learning, deep learning, LLM, NLP, generative AI, RAG

OVERVIEW:
This course introduces artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and generative AI. Students learn to build AI-powered applications and chatbots.

MODULES:
1. AI Fundamentals: AI concepts, applications, and intelligent systems.
2. Machine Learning: Supervised learning, unsupervised learning, classification, and regression.
3. Deep Learning: Neural networks, training, and model evaluation.
4. NLP: Text processing, sentiment analysis, and language models.
5. Generative AI: LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI APIs.
6. RAG: Embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, and document question answering.
7. AI Projects: Chatbots, document assistants, and responsible AI.

PREREQUISITES:
Basic computer knowledge is recommended. Python programming experience is helpful.

PROJECTS:
- AI-powered FAQ chatbot
- Sentiment analysis application
- Document question-answering system
- RAG-based institute assistant

FAQ:
Q: What is the course duration?
A: 16 weeks.

Q: What is RAG?
A: Retrieval-Augmented Generation retrieves relevant external information and provides it to a language model to generate context-grounded answers.

Q: Does the course guarantee employment?
A: No. Employment depends on skills, experience, and employer requirements.
